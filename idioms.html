Spill the beans

To give away a secret, usually before you meant to.

She spilled the beans about the surprise picnic before the basket was even packed.

把秘密说漏了，而且常常是还没打算说的时候。

野餐篮子还没装好，她就把惊喜野餐的事说漏嘴了。

Piece of cake

Something easy, the sort of task that barely needs both hands.

The first puzzle was a piece of cake. The second one had opinions.

形容事情很容易，轻松就能做成。

第一道谜题不过是小菜一碟。第二道却很有自己的主意。

Under the weather

Feeling unwell, and not in the mood for a parade.

He stayed in, under the weather, with tea and a very dramatic blanket.

身体不舒服，也没心情出去凑热闹。

他不舒服，留在家里，捧着茶，裹着一条格外夸张的毯子。

When pigs fly

Something so unlikely you can schedule it next to never.

I will fold that map correctly when pigs fly, and not a minute sooner.

形容根本不可能发生的事。

除非猪会飞，我才会把那张地图折对，一分钟都不会提前。

Break the ice

To say or do the thing that lets a conversation finally start.

A terrible pun is a reliable way to break the ice, and also to thin the crowd.

说一句或做一件事，让谈话总算开了头。

一个糟糕的双关语既能打破冷场，也能把人吓跑。

Burn the midnight oil

To keep working long after the sensible people have gone to bed.

She burned the midnight oil, and the oil, to its credit, did not complain.

别人都睡了，还在继续干活。

她开夜车开到很晚，那盏灯倒很识趣，一声没抱怨。

Hold your horses

To slow down, wait a moment, and curb your enthusiasm.

Hold your horses—we cannot leave for the picnic until someone actually finds the basket.

先慢下来，等一等，别那么急。

先别急——得有人找到篮子，我们才能出发去野餐。

Barking up the wrong tree

Looking in the wrong place, or pursuing a mistaken line of thought.

If you think I took the last cookie, you are barking up the wrong tree—and the jar was empty yesterday.

找错了地方，或者想偏了。

要是你以为最后那块饼干是我拿的，那你就找错人了——罐子昨天就空了。
